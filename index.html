How to Play

1

The auction begins

Players come up one by one. On your turn, bid or pass — the highest bidder signs that player.

2

Build your squad

Collect 5 players with a $20 budget: 2 Forwards · 2 Midfielders · 1 Defender. Spend wisely — don't run out.

3

Win

When squads are full, total ratings are compared. The stronger XI wins the match.
